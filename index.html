🌍 What is a carbon footprint?

A carbon footprint is how much greenhouse gas (mostly CO₂) we put into the air from things we do — car rides, electricity, the food we eat, the stuff we buy.

More CO₂ traps more heat, which makes summers hotter. Smaller footprint = cooler, happier planet.

📏 The unit: how we measure it

CO₂ is measured by weight, in grams (g) and kilograms (kg).

1,000 g = 1 kg1,000 kg = 1 tonne

One tree soaks up roughly 20 kg of CO₂ a year — that's why trees matter!
