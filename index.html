🌍 What is a carbon footprint?

A carbon footprint is how much greenhouse gas (mostly CO₂) we put into the air — from car rides, electricity, food, and the stuff we buy.

More CO₂ traps more heat, making summers hotter. Smaller footprint = cooler, happier planet.

📏 How we measure it

CO₂ is measured in grams (g) and kilograms (kg).

1,000 g = 1 kg1,000 kg = 1 tonne

One tree soaks up about 20 kg of CO₂ a year — that's why the tree in the app grows with your good actions!
