🌍 What is a carbon footprint?

A carbon footprint is how much greenhouse gas (mostly CO₂) we put into the air from car rides, electricity, food, and shopping.

CO₂ acts like a blanket around Earth — too much traps heat, making Hong Kong summers hotter and typhoons stronger.

🌡️Earth is now hotter by+1.1°C
🌊Sea level rising~3mm per year
🌪️Typhoons gettingstronger & wetter

📏 How we measure CO₂

CO₂ is measured by weight in grams (g) and kilograms (kg).

1,000 g = 1 kg1,000 kg = 1 tonne
🌳1 tree absorbs per year~20 kg CO₂
🏙️Hong Kong emits per year~40 million tonnes
🧍Average HK person per year~5.5 tonnes

⚖️ Transport comparisons

🚗Private car or taxi~150 g per km
🚌Bus (shared)~40 g per km
🚇MTR (very efficient)~10 g per km
🚲Bike or walking~0 g
✈️HK → Tokyo flight~400 kg per person!

Taking the MTR instead of a taxi for one week saves the same CO₂ as planting 2 trees! 🌳🌳

🍽️ Food and the planet

What we eat matters just as much as how we travel. Beef needs enormous amounts of land, water and energy.

🥩Beef (per kg produced)~60 kg CO₂
🐔Chicken (per kg)~6 kg CO₂
🥦Vegetables (per kg)~2 kg CO₂
🗑️Wasted food globally8% of all emissions!

One veggie meal a week instead of beef for a year saves as much CO₂ as driving 500 km!

👗 Fast fashion facts

The fashion industry makes 10% of global CO₂ emissions — more than all international flights combined!

👕1 cotton T-shirt uses water2,700 litres!
👖1 pair of jeans CO₂~33 kg CO₂
♻️Buying secondhand saves82% less CO₂

Most cheap clothes are worn only 7 times before being thrown away. Wearing clothes longer is one of the easiest ways to help!

🌊 Oceans and plastic

Oceans absorb 25% of all CO₂ humans produce — but plastic is threatening the tiny plants that do this work.

🐢Plastic enters ocean per year~8 million tonnes
🧴A plastic bottle lasts~450 years
🐟Fish found with plastic insideover 50% studied

Hong Kong's beaches collect some of the most plastic per km² in Asia. A reusable bottle is one of the easiest swaps!

⚡ Energy at home

Most of Hong Kong's electricity comes from burning gas and coal. Every light or AC you leave on adds carbon to the air.

❄️AC running for 1 hour~1 kg CO₂
💡LED vs old light bulb75% less energy
📱Charging a phone~8 g CO₂
🚿Hot shower (5 min)~700 g CO₂

Setting AC to 25°C instead of 20°C cuts energy use by nearly half!

🐧 Animals in danger

Climate change is disrupting habitats faster than many animals can adapt. Some are already disappearing.

🐻‍❄️Polar bearsLosing sea ice home
🐠Coral reefs50% lost since 1950
🦋ButterfliesMoving to cooler areas
🐸FrogsMost threatened vertebrate

Hong Kong is home to 236 butterfly species and many migratory birds — all sensitive to temperature changes.

🌱 Nature's carbon sinks

Some natural systems absorb huge amounts of CO₂ — they're called carbon sinks. We must protect them!

🌳Forests worldwide absorb2.6 billion tonnes/year
🌊Oceans absorb25% of our CO₂
🌿Mangroves store10x more than forests

Hong Kong has mangrove forests in Tai Po and Sai Kung. They protect coastlines AND store carbon!

🔎 Be a number detective

Every number in this app is a rough estimate. Real scientists always check: where did this number come from?

Challenge: search "how much CO₂ does a beef burger produce?" and compare with this app. Who is right — and how do you know? Check at least 3 sources!
